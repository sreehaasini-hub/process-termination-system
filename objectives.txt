PROJECT OBJECTIVES

1. To create multiple child processes using fork().

2. To assign different tasks to each child process.

3. To monitor the termination of child processes.

4. To use wait() for collecting child termination status.

5. To identify normally terminated child processes.

6. To identify child processes terminated by signals.

7. To display the PID of the terminated child process.

8. To display the exit status of normally terminated processes.

9. To report the signal responsible for abnormal termination.

10. To prevent terminated child processes from becoming zombie processes.
